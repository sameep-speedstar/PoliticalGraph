AI consulting — common questions

What does an AI consulting and implementation partner do?

An AI consulting and implementation partner helps organizations adopt AI in production — assessing where AI adds value, building agents, workflows and integrations, and operating those systems reliably over time. KNIQ does all three: consulting, implementation, and managed services.

What's the difference between AI consulting and implementation?

AI consulting identifies where AI can replace manual or judgment-heavy work and designs the solution and roadmap. AI implementation is the build: developing the agents, integrations and pipelines, and shipping them to production.

Which AI models and tech stacks do you work with?

We are model-fluent and stack-pragmatic. We ship with Claude, OpenAI, and other frontier models as the problem demands, plus TypeScript/Node, Next.js, Python, Postgres, MCP, WhatsApp/Telegram channels, Cloudflare, and Docker. We are also a Claude Partner Network member.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide, from startups to enterprises. Being based in India lets us deliver the same quality as global peers at a more competitive price. We have particular depth in finance, audit and other regulated domains, but we build across industries.

How do we get started with an AI project?

Book a free consultation. We start with a short discovery to map the workflow worth automating, then propose a scoped engagement — usually a focused build that ships to production, followed by managed iteration.
