Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Speedstar AI Labs Private Limited ("Speedstar AI Labs", "KNIQ", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you visit www.kniq.ai (the "Site") or contact us through it.

We are a company incorporated in India, operating the KNIQ consulting practice from Bangalore, Karnataka.

Scope. This policy covers (a) your use of the Site, and (b) LifeOS, our AI chief-of-staff product offered under KNIQ, which is described in detail in Section 10. Our other products are covered by their own policies.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

Data controller / Data Fiduciary: Speedstar AI Labs Private Limited, Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

Privacy contact: sales@speedstar.ai

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, email the address above with "Privacy" in the subject line.

2. What personal data we collect

a. Information you give us

When you submit our enquiry form, or contact us by email, WhatsApp, or by booking a call, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name (optional)
  • The nature of your interest in our services
  • The content of your message, and any information you include in it

Please do not send us confidential, sensitive, or special-category information through the enquiry form. If a project requires it, we will agree an appropriate, secure channel with you first.

b. Information collected automatically

When you browse the Site, our analytics and hosting providers may collect standard technical information, including your IP address (which may be truncated or anonymised by the provider), device and browser type, approximate location derived from your IP, pages viewed, referring source, and interactions such as submitting the form or clicking a contact link.

We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

c. What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not collect payment or financial information through the Site. We do not sell personal data, and we do not run advertising on the Site.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand aggregate traffic and which pages and contact options people use. We use it to measure page views and a small number of interaction events, such as a completed enquiry or a click on a contact link.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf, and only as needed to run the Site and respond to you:

We may also disclose data where required by law, to comply with legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We are based in India, and the providers listed above may process data on servers located in other countries, including outside your own. If you would like more detail about a specific provider, contact us.

7. How long we keep data

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our business discussions, and thereafter only as long as required for legitimate business or legal purposes. Analytics data is retained according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics. If you ask us to delete your enquiry data, we will do so unless we are required to keep it by law.

8. How we protect data

The Site is served over HTTPS. We apply access controls and follow the principle of collecting only what we need. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we take reasonable and appropriate measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. LifeOS

LifeOS is an AI chief-of-staff product built by Speedstar AI Labs Private Limited. It is currently in closed testing with a small group of individual users (the founder and up to five invited testers). This section describes how LifeOS specifically collects, uses, and protects personal data, in addition to the general commitments above. Where this section and the general sections differ, this section governs LifeOS.

10.1 Google user data — Limited Use commitment

LifeOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms, this means:

  • We use your Google data only to provide the user-facing features of LifeOS described below.
  • We do not transfer or sell your Google data, and we do not use it for advertising of any kind.
  • We do not use your Google data to train, fine-tune, or improve any generative AI or machine-learning model, and we do not build databases or permanent copies of it beyond what is described in "Retention" below.
  • No human reads your Google data, except where you give us specific consent (for example, to debug an issue you have reported), where it is necessary for security purposes, or where we are required to do so by law.

10.2 What LifeOS accesses

Only with your explicit connection and consent, LifeOS accesses:

  • Gmail — message headers, snippets, and extracted fields such as sender, subject, money amounts, and deadlines. LifeOS does not read full message bodies unless a briefing you have requested requires it.
  • Google Calendar — your events, for reading, and (only after your explicit confirmation) for writing changes you have asked for.
  • Notes, reminders, and voice-note transcripts that you send to LifeOS directly via WhatsApp or Telegram. Voice audio itself is deleted immediately after transcription; only the text transcript is kept.

10.3 Why LifeOS accesses it

This access powers LifeOS's core function, and nothing else: surfacing what needs your attention across your email and calendar, capturing your notes and reminders, and assembling a morning and evening briefing for you.

10.4 How it is protected

  • Every user's data is isolated by account. There is no code path that reads across users.
  • Google account tokens are encrypted at rest, and all traffic is protected in transit using TLS.
  • Every write LifeOS makes to your Google account is recorded in an audit trail.
  • LifeOS writes to your Google Calendar only after you explicitly confirm the specific action (for example, replying "OK" to a proposed change). It never acts on your accounts silently.

10.5 Your control

  • Ask LifeOS "what do you know about me" to see what it has inferred and stored about you.
  • Say "forget X" to remove a specific fact.
  • Send "pause" to stop all activity.
  • Send "delete my data" to permanently erase your account data, connected tokens, and history.
  • You can also disconnect LifeOS from your Google Account at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

Each tester signs a short, plain-language consent note before onboarding, describing exactly what is accessed, what is stored, and how to leave.

10.6 Retention

Content is kept only as long as your account is active. Deleting your account (see above) removes it immediately, subject only to what we are legally required to retain.

10.7 Contact

Privacy questions specific to LifeOS can be sent to sales@speedstar.ai with "LifeOS" in the subject line.
